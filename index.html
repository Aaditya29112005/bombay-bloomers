Flavours from every corner of India

Punjabi tandoor, Kashmiri rogan josh, Goan vindaloo, Kerala coconut curries and crisp South Indian dosa, all from one kitchen in Randwick.

Bombay Bloomers first opened its doors on Perouse Road in Randwick and has fed the neighbourhood since 1987. In 2019 we moved to The Spot, Randwick, where you will find us today. The recipes have been passed down through generations, cooked by a team of seasoned chefs who balance fresh ingredients with a deep shelf of spices.

Pull up a seat at the bar, too. We pour our own signature cocktails, classics, mocktails, Kingfisher on tap and a wine list from Australia, New Zealand and France, made to go with a plate of something from the tandoor.

Today the same menu and the same warmth also live across the bridge in Neutral Bay. Two rooms with one way of cooking: do it properly and share it generously.
